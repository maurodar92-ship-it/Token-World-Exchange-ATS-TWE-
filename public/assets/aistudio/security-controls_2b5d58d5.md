# Security Control Baseline

Identity: MFA, least privilege, RBAC, privileged access management.

Keys: HSM/MPC, dual control, quorum approvals, rotation, recovery ceremonies.

Application: secure SDLC, pinned dependencies, SAST/DAST, review, secrets scanning, SBOM, signed releases.

Market integrity: self-trade prevention, fat-finger limits, price collars, duplicate controls, throttling, kill switch, halt controls, surveillance.

Infrastructure: segmentation, WAF, DDoS protection, centralized logging, SIEM, encrypted backups, immutable audit logs, disaster recovery.

Testing: unit/integration tests, adversarial market simulations, penetration tests, smart-contract audit, recovery and failover exercises.
