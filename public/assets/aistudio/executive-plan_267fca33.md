# TOKEN WORLD — Executive Strategic Plan

## Mission
Create regulated infrastructure for compliant tokenization, primary issuance administration, and secondary-market liquidity for eligible private-market and real-world assets.

## Positioning
Token World is envisioned as market infrastructure, not an unlicensed replacement for a national securities exchange.

## Business lines
Issuer services; token lifecycle; investor onboarding; private-market trading infrastructure; institutional APIs; settlement orchestration; compliance technology; market data; reporting.

## Revenue concepts
Issuer onboarding, platform subscriptions, transaction fees, data/API, enterprise integration, and administration/settlement fees where legally permitted. Each requires legal review.

## Budget categories
Legal/regulatory; broker-dealer/FINRA; compliance/AML; cybersecurity; cloud; custody/transfer agent; insurance; audits; surveillance; accounting/tax; support; liquidity arrangements.
