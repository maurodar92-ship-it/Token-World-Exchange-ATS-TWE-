# Settlement Lifecycle

1. Account onboarding and eligibility.
2. Security approval and token-rule loading.
3. Buyer/seller order submission.
4. Pre-trade controls: account, security, investor category, jurisdiction, restrictions, position/cash, sanctions/AML, price/size limits.
5. Matching and execution.
6. Authoritative trade capture.
7. Settlement instructions to approved custodian/settlement agent.
8. DvP under the approved legal/custody model.
9. Reconciliation of token ledger, transfer-agent records, custody records, and cash.
10. Exception workflow for failed settlement.
11. Regulatory and management reporting.

A successful blockchain transaction is not by itself proof of legal transfer of a security. The legal and operational records must be mapped explicitly.
