# U.S. Regulatory Map — Initial Workplan

## 1. Decide what Token World legally is

Counsel should issue a classification memo addressing security status, issuer-vs-third-party tokenization, asset type, offering pathway, secondary trading restrictions, investor eligibility, custody, settlement, and whether the system is an ATS, exchange, broker-dealer system, or another regulated activity.

## 2. ATS pathway

An ATS generally operates within Regulation ATS and is not itself an independent exchange license. The operator must satisfy applicable broker-dealer/ATS requirements and file the appropriate report. Form ATS is used for applicable ATS initial operation, amendments, and cessation. NMS Stock ATSs have additional Form ATS-N requirements.

## 3. Broker-dealer / FINRA

Establish the correct legal entity and pursue registration/membership or a regulated-partner structure as advised by securities counsel. Address capital, supervision, AML/BSA, customer protection, books and records, cybersecurity, and operational controls.

## 4. Offerings

Regulation D, Regulation S, Rule 144A, and Regulation A can involve different eligibility, disclosure, filing, solicitation, and resale requirements. They are not interchangeable and none is an ATS license.

## 5. Custody / settlement

Define who controls the security, private keys, customer assets, cash, settlement instructions, and corporate records. Do not use the demo contract as a substitute for regulated custody or transfer-agent infrastructure.

## 6. Records and surveillance

Production needs immutable order/trade records, synchronized clocks, audit trails, supervisory review, market-abuse surveillance, access controls, retention, BCP/DR, reconciliation, and regulatory reporting.

## 7. NYSE relationship

Token World cannot become a NYSE market by using the NYSE name or by deploying tokens. A public listing or relationship with NYSE/ICE requires a separate commercial and regulatory process with the relevant entities.
