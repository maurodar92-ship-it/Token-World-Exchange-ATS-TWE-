# Strategic Build Phases

0. Legal/business definition — classification memo, target assets, entity/regulatory map.
1. Corporate formation/governance — entity, capitalization, policies, vendor plan.
2. Regulated-partner architecture — broker-dealer/ATS, custody, transfer agent, banking/escrow, KYC/AML, settlement.
3. Security/offering framework — token terms, offering pathway, disclosures, eligibility, restrictions.
4. Sandbox technology — matching engine, APIs, portal, permissioned-token demo, audit logs.
5. Independent assurance — smart-contract audit, pen test, controls testing.
6. Regulatory filing/review — applicable registrations/filings, FINRA/SRO work, legal opinions.
7. Controlled pilot — limited participants/instruments and reconciliation/surveillance evidence.
8. Production launch — approved procedures, infrastructure, support, reporting.
9. Scale — issuers, institutions, custody/TA integrations, surveillance, international expansion.
10. Public-market strategy — separate analysis for any public listing or exchange relationship.
