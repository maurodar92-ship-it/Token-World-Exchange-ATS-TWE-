# Regulatory Document Index / Data Room

Corporate: formation documents; bylaws/operating agreement; ownership/cap table; board resolutions; conflicts; related-party policy; delegation matrix.

Broker-dealer/ATS: Form BD workplan; FINRA NMA workplan; Form ATS workbook; ATS-N applicability assessment; ATS-R reporting workbook; supervisory procedures; BCP; cybersecurity; books/records matrix; subscriber agreement; fair-access policy; confidential trading information; system integrity/security; surveillance; error trades; kill switch; incident response.

Issuance: offering memorandum/PPM; subscription agreement; investor questionnaire; eligibility procedures; QIB/offshore verification where applicable; legends/restrictions; token terms; risk factors; transfer-agent agreement; custody; escrow; settlement.

Technology: architecture; threat model; data flows; key-management; audit report; pen test; DR test; change management; SBOM; vendor risk.

These are templates/checklists, not legal filings. Professionals must finalize them.
